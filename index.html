MailCraft — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · App: MailCraft (com.shrijay.mailcraft)

In short: MailCraft has no servers, no ads and no analytics. Your templates, contacts lists (CSV files) and drafts stay on your phone. Emails leave your phone only when you send them, either through your own Gmail account (Gmail API) or through a mail app you choose. The developer never receives your emails or data.

1. Templates, drafts and recipient lists

Email templates, drafts, photos you add, scheduled emails and CSV recipient lists you import are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. MailCraft reads a CSV or image file only when you pick it.

2. Sending directly through Gmail (optional)

If you add a Google account in Settings, MailCraft asks Google for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send ("Send email on your behalf"). MailCraft uses it only to send the emails you compose, schedule or bulk-send. It cannot read, delete or modify your mailbox. The email (recipients, subject, body and attachments) is sent over HTTPS directly from your phone to Google's Gmail API. Google's handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in MailCraft Settings.

MailCraft's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never transferred to the developer or any third party, never used for advertising, and never read by humans.

3. Sending through a mail app

If you choose "Mail app", MailCraft fills in the email and hands it to the app you pick (for example Gmail or Outlook). You send it from that app, under that app's privacy policy.

5. What we do not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No developer server: the developer cannot see your emails, templates, contacts or usage.
  • No selling or sharing of data. The app is not directed at children under 13.

6. Deleting your data

Delete templates, drafts and lists in the app, remove your Google account in Settings, or uninstall the app to delete all local data. Emails you already sent remain in your Gmail "Sent" folder and are managed by Google.
